Matrubhasha Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Matrubhasha (मातृभाषा) is a picture book of first words for young children. We built it so that it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, send or share any personal information about you or your child. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code.

No internet connection

The app makes no network requests. Every picture and sound is either built into the app or produced on your device. The spoken words are recordings built into the app.

Settings on your device

The app remembers a few choices on your device only: the selected language, voice, tap mode and whether to show Roman spelling. These never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

The app is made for children under 5 and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's Kids Category rules. Settings are behind a parent gate. There are no links out of the app and no purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above before releasing a version of the app that the change affects.
