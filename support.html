Matrubhasha Support

Matrubhasha (मातृभाषा) is a tap-and-hear picture book of 90 first words in Hindi, for toddlers. Tap a picture to hear it. The four buttons at the bottom choose what you hear: the word, a sentence, something to do, or something to discover.

I can't hear anything

Turn up the volume. The app plays sound even when the silent switch is on. If the words are silent, check that a Hindi voice is available: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Hindi.

The voice sounds robotic

Download a better Hindi voice on your device: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → Hindi, then choose an Enhanced or Premium voice. The app uses the best one installed.

Settings

Tap the gear at the top right and answer the sum. You can show Roman spelling under each word there.
